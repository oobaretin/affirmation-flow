AffirmEaze Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

AffirmEaze is a local-first affirmation app. Your data stays on your device unless you optionally use the AI Generator with your own OpenAI API key.

On-device data

  • Name, preferences, custom affirmations, favorites, streaks
  • Local notification schedule

Optional third-party AI

If you add an OpenAI API key, intention text is sent to OpenAI for generation. Without a key, templates run locally only.
