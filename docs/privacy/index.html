AffirmEaze Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Overview

AffirmEaze is a subscription-based affirmation app. Your affirmations, favorites, settings, and practice data are stored on your device — not on our servers.

Data we store on your device

  • Your name and practice preferences
  • Custom and favorite affirmations
  • Daily streak and pinned affirmation choices
  • Notification schedule preferences

Subscriptions

AffirmEaze Premium is required to use the app. Payments are processed by Apple through In-App Purchase. We use RevenueCat to verify subscription status on your device. We do not store your payment card details.

Data that may leave your device

Affirmation generator: Suggestions are created on your device from your selected category. This text is not sent to our servers.

Share: When you share an affirmation, your device's share sheet or clipboard is used. We do not receive shared content.

Voice: Spoken affirmations use premium voice synthesis (ElevenLabs). Affirmation text is sent to ElevenLabs to generate audio. Audio is played on your device and is not stored on our servers.

Notifications

If enabled, AffirmEaze schedules local reminders on your device. We do not send push notifications from a remote server.

Account & cloud sync

AffirmEaze does not require an account and does not offer cloud backup in v1. Locking the app only pauses your session on this device.

Children's privacy

AffirmEaze is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
